# Create Agreement Surface

- Mode: Operate
- Job: Turn a service brief into a funded onchain agreement with minimal hesitation.
- Primary action: Approve USDC, then create agreement.
- Required information: work title, beneficiary, arbiter, amount, deadline, acceptance criteria, optional notes.
- Direction: A concise digital work order with a sticky readiness summary.
- Memorable moment: The settlement amount and transaction readiness remain visible while the agreement is written.
